## BoxyCatAdventure: Privacy policy

This is privacy policy for BoxyCatAdventure Android game

### Data collected by the app

I hereby state, to the best of my knowledge and belief, that I have not programmed this app to collect any personally identifiable information. All data created by you (the user) is stored locally in your device only, and can be simply erased by clearing the app's data or uninstalling it. No analytics software is present in the app either.

### Explanation of permissions requested in the app

The list of permissions required by the app can be found in the `AndroidManifest.xml` file:

| Permission | Why it is required |
| :---: | --- |
| `android.permission.WRITE_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | For writing scoreboard information
| `android.permission.READ_EXTERNAL_STORAGE` | For reading scoreboard information
| `android.permission.INTERNET` | Required to load and display advertisements from Google AdMob
| `android.permission.ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | Allows the app to check whether an internet connection is available before attempting to load ads. This helps avoid unnecessary failed requests
| `android.hardware.touchscreen` | and related multitouch features – Required for touch input and gameplay
| `android.hardware.vulkan.version` | Used for improved graphics rendering on supported devices.
